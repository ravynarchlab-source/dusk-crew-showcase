# Press

**Dusk Crew** is a first-person co-op horror game by RAVYN, for one or two players, on Windows PC. A free demo comes to itch.io when it is ready. There is no fixed date.

## Use of media

You may use the images, GIFs and clips on this page and in the [Footage WIP release](https://github.com/ravynarchlab-source/dusk-crew-showcase/releases/tag/footage-wip) in coverage of Dusk Crew. Please credit RAVYN.

- Everything is work in progress (WIP) and will change.
- Spoilers are kept behind a warning. Please keep the creature's look and the Returned out of headlines and thumbnails.
- The footage has no sound.

## Short description

Every light in the valley went out. You are the line crew sent back to turn them on. By day you build light. By night you walk out of it to bring people home. Something is watching from the dark.

## Fact sheet
